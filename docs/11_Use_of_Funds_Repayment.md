# Use of Funds & Repayment Plan: Numero Sansar

This document outlines the strategic allocation of the requested bank loan and the plan for sustainable repayment.

## 1. Use of Funds Breakdown
The requested capital will be allocated to accelerate product maturity and market penetration:

-   **Product Development (40%)**:
    -   Finalizing the Native Mobile App (iOS/Android).
    -   Implementing the Gemini AI-driven report engine.
    -   Expanding the B2B Practitioner CRM features.
-   **Marketing & Customer Acquisition (35%)**:
    -   Auspicious branding SEO and content strategy.
    -   Paid acquisition campaigns in target regions (India/UK/Germany).
-   **Infrastructure & Security (15%)**:
    -   Scaling cloud infrastructure for global reach.
    -   Security audits and data protection compliance.
-   **Working Capital (10%)**:
    -   General operations and regional market research.

## 2. Revenue Streams (Repayment Sources)
Repayment will be supported by diversified revenue streams:
-   **High-Margin Digital Sales**: Premium PDF reports ($10–$20 each) with 95%+ profit margin.
-   **Stable Recurring Revenue**: SaaS subscriptions from professional practitioners.
-   **License Fees**: Quarterly payments from B2B white-label partners.

## 3. Repayment Strategy
-   **Grace Period**: Requested 6-month grace period on principal repayment during the core development phase.
-   **Installments**: Monthly repayments based on projected cash flow, starting from Month 7.
-   **Early Repayment**: Option to settle the loan early as revenue scales beyond Year 2 projections.

## 4. Financial Security
The project's low overhead and high-margin digital products ensure a strong Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), providing the bank with a high level of security.
